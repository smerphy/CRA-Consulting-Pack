# Independent verification of the CRA knowledge base — 30 October 2026

Scope: the 32 high-stakes claims supplied by the author. Verified against primary sources where reachable. EUR-Lex HTML/PDF for the CRA itself returned only the recitals (text truncated at ~165k characters; direct downloads returned HTTP 202 bot-challenge pages), so for the CRA articles and annexes two independent verbatim mirrors were compared: enobyte.com/en/legal/cra/{n}/ and springlex.eu (articles), plus streamlex.eu (annexes). Both mirrors agreed on every passage quoted below. Everything else was checked on the issuing body's own page (EUR-Lex OJ HTML for secondary acts, Commission digital-strategy pages, ENISA, BSI, UK Parliament Bills API, legislation.gov.uk, HM Treasury, Law Commission, Council document register, legislation.mt, Finlex) with secondary sources only where the primary was unreachable or silent.

## Corrections that matter most

1. **Claim 28 (EUCC register) — figures stale.** The ENISA register on 30 Oct 2026 shows **111 valid certificates (112 total, 1 withdrawn) issued by 9 certification bodies**, not 103 from 8. Substantial-level: 19 (18 valid). First substantial certificate (DEKRA, GMV GNSS Cryptographic Module, 23 Jul 2025) and the planned Q4 2026 Art 27(9) delegated act are correct.
2. **Claim 24 (Germany) — date.** The Bundestag plenary passed the CRA-Durchführungsgesetz on **Thursday 15 October 2026**; 14 October 2026 was the Innenausschuss vote (hib 822/2026; Drucksache 21/8622). BSI's 16 Oct release refers to "gestrige Verabschiedung". Add: Bundesrat consent not required; promulgation pending.
3. **Claim 7 (Art 14) — legal basis.** The delegated act on delaying dissemination (now Del. Reg. 2026/881) is empowered by **Art 14(9)**, which cross-refers to the grounds in Art 16(2); Art 16(2) itself is not the empowering provision. Art 14(10) for the implementing act is correct.
4. **Claim 5 (Art 24)** — reporting obligations are in **paragraph 3** (Article 24 has three paragraphs); para 3 covers Art 14(1) for AEVs and Art 14(3)/(8) for severe incidents affecting the steward's development infrastructure.
5. **Claim 15 (Commission guidance)** — keep stating that C(2026) 5252 is an *approved draft* awaiting formal adoption and that **no formal adoption or OJ C publication was found as of 30 Oct 2026** (Commission pages last updated 7 Sep 2026 still say "published guidance" only).
6. **Claim 18 (AI Act Art 42(3))** — precision: deemed compliance with Art 15 applies where "the conditions laid down in Article 12(1) of [the CRA] are fulfilled", not merely for "CRA-compliant products".
7. **Claim 20 (Digital Omnibus)** — the Council mandate (ST 14756/26) drops the Commission's ENISA single-entry point in favour of **national entry points plus an ENISA "incident reporting information point"**, and would deem CRA Art 14(3) severe-incident reports to satisfy NIS2 Art 23(4). The KB's "proposes a NIS2 single entry point" describes the Commission text, not the Council position.
8. **Claim 22 (standards)** — the 2 Nov 2026 ratification date for EN 40000-1-1/-1-2, the 3 Aug 2026 HAS submission for prEN 40000-1-3 and the per-item M/606 deadlines rest on secondary trackers; iTeh shows EN 40000-1-1:2026 at "Closure of Vote - Formal Approval" with publication 17 Nov 2026. Label them as such. The draft amending decision's horizontal date is reported as 30 Oct 2026 by some and 31 Oct 2026 by others.
9. **Claim 29 (CVE)** — 20 CNAs as of 6 Aug 2026 is correct, but ENISA announced further CNAs on 21 Oct 2026; refresh the number.

10. **Addendum 1 Nov 2026 (Enterprise module E6, correction C-10): placing of software versions.** Sections 03 and the application guide 11 said placing is "per version" for standalone software, which three Enterprise modules read as making every version a new placing. The guidance says otherwise: all copies of a version are placed at its first offering, later versions keep that date unless they are a substantial modification (para 15, Example 2), and builds for different platforms are distinct products (para 14). The KB text now says so (sections/03-scope.md, the combined document and guides/application/11-documentation-set.md). Source: C(2026) 5252 annex, Commission PDF of 27 Jul 2026 (copy and hash in pack/enterprise/oj-verification/source/guidance/MANIFEST.csv).
