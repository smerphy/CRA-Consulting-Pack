**Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act): request for confirmation on the structure of Article 14 notifications**

Dear Sir or Madam,

Aldermoor Ireland Ltd is the main establishment in the Union of the Aldermoor group for the purposes of Article 14(7) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, because decisions on the cybersecurity of the group's products with digital elements are predominantly taken in Dublin. We therefore submit our Article 14 notifications to the NCSC as the CSIRT designated as coordinator, through the ENISA Single Reporting Platform (SRP).

We have read the NCSC's National CRA Guidelines of 31 August 2026, the ENISA SRP FAQ of 15 October 2026, the Commission FAQ (version 1.4) and the Commission guidance approved on 27 July 2026 (C(2026) 5252). We note that the NCSC does not advise on product scope or general applicability, and we have limited this letter to how notifications are structured and routed. We would be grateful for the NCSC's confirmation, or its view, on the three questions below.

**1. One notification for several products affected by the same vulnerability or incident**

Several of our products share code. A single actively exploited vulnerability in a shared component can be present and exploitable in up to six separately placed products (for example a browser on two platforms, an embedded browser component, a laptop operating system, a desktop client and a smart display), each with its own release train and its own corrective measure. Likewise, one severe incident in a shared back-end service may affect several products.

ENISA's FAQ 8 states that "Only one notification is required for any given actively exploited vulnerability (AEV) or severe incident (SI)", in the context of a manufacturer's corporate structure. Neither the ENISA FAQ nor the Commission guidance addresses several products of one manufacturer.

(a) Does the NCSC accept one notification that lists every affected product and version, with the Member States for each product and each product's corrective measure, in place of one notification per product?

(b) If so, should the final report under Article 14(2)(c) be submitted 14 days after the first product's corrective measure is available, with later measures added by update, or 14 days after the last?

(c) If not, is a shared case reference in the title and summary of each per-product notification helpful to the NCSC?

Until we receive the NCSC's view we submit one notification per affected product, each cross-referenced to the others.

**2. A legal person that is both manufacturer and open-source software steward for the same code**

The group publishes open-source projects that it does not monetise and for which it expects to act as open-source software steward within the meaning of Article 3(14). It also places on the market products that integrate the same code. From 11 December 2027, Article 24(3) will apply Article 14(1) to stewards to the extent that they are involved in development.

(a) Where the same legal person is manufacturer of the affected products and steward of the affected project, does the NCSC expect one notification identifying both capacities, or separate notifications as manufacturer and as steward?

(b) Where exploitation is known only in a third party's product built on the project, we understand that a notification is due in the steward capacity only. Is that the NCSC's understanding?

(c) Can the same Assigned Representatives be associated in the SRP with both the manufacturer and the steward, or does each capacity need its own association?

**3. Coordinator for steward notifications**

We intend to name Aldermoor Ireland Ltd as the steward entity for the projects concerned. Can the NCSC confirm that it would be the CSIRT designated as coordinator for that entity's notifications as steward, applying Article 14(7) through Article 24(3)?

We would welcome a written reply, and we are glad to meet the NCSC to explain the products and code-sharing involved. We will treat any reply as the NCSC's view at the date given and not as a decision binding on any authority. Please contact me at [email] or [telephone].
