# Enterprise Edition — master brief (all module writers read this first)

## Why
The base pack scores ~5/10 for a hyperscale technology company (feedback: built for 13 families and spreadsheets; misses build-generated evidence, Art 14 at volume, portfolio-wide classification, open-source roles, multi-jurisdiction control set, and OJ-verified legal text). The Enterprise Edition fixes this with six modules plus a Google outside-in appendix. Target reader: CTO, CISO, General Counsel, VP Engineering of a hyperscaler. Standard: what a top-tier firm's technology practice would hand a Big Tech CTO — specific, quantified, engineering-literate, honest about uncertainty.

## Modules (folder under pack/enterprise/)
- E1 `evidence-as-code/` — per-version CRA evidence generated by the build/release pipeline (spec + working reference implementation + CI examples).
- E2 `art14-operations/` — Article 14 reporting at volume: operating model, decision rights, case engine (working code), volume and staffing model.
- E3 `portfolio-classification/` — portfolio-wide classification and conformity-route map with notified-body demand model.
- E4 `open-source-strategy/` — steward vs manufacturer roles; downstream reliance; policy paper.
- E5 `global-control-set/` — one control set mapped to EU CRA, UK PSTI, US Cyber Trust Mark, Japan JC-STAR (and optionally Singapore CLS / Australia) with an interactive page.
- E6 `oj-verification/` — every legal claim in the pack verified against the Official Journal text of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847; claims register with status.
- G `google-outside-in/` — applies E2-E5 to Google using public sources only.
Each module writes `paper.md` (the deliverable text, 1,500-4,000 words, structure below) plus its data/code. A coordinator later assembles the papers into one Claude Doc and the portal.

## Paper structure (paper.md)
1. Title (H1) and a one-paragraph executive answer (the "so what", with numbers).
2. Sections with action titles (full-sentence headings stating the point).
3. Exhibits as pipe tables (and Mermaid in ```mermaid blocks only where a flow/architecture is the point).
4. "Decisions for the executive team" (3-6 numbered, each with owner role and timing).
5. "Risks and open questions".
6. "Sources" — every external fact linked; KB facts cite the knowledge-base section (reports/sections/NN-*.md) or verification.md.
Style: British English; short plain sentences; no hype, no emoji, no em-dash asides; roles not names; numbers with units; label estimates as estimates; facts as at 1 Nov 2026 (regulatory facts verified to 30 Oct 2026; notified-body status at checks to 8 Sep 2026 unless you verify newer).
