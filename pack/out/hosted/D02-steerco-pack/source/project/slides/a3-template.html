Project Rampart · SteerCo #1 · 30 Oct 2026

Illustrative · A3

This page makes the pack reusable. Every number has one system of record and one owner, so each month is a refresh, not a rewrite. Five working days before SteerCo, owners refresh their systems; the PMO updates tables and charts; leads confirm RAG and notes; the sponsor reviews and drafts decisions. Keep KPI, risk and task IDs and row order stable; move closed items to the log, and update the cover date and footers.
