Illustrative · 5

The dates are fixed in the regulation. It entered into force in December 2024. The rules for notified bodies have applied since June 2026, yet none had been verifiably designated at the last check. Reporting has applied since 11 September 2026, and it covers every in-scope product we have ever placed on the market, not only new ones. In three weeks the new Product Liability Directive applies, which treats software as a product. From 11 December 2027 every unit we place on the EU market must conform. That is 387 days from today.
