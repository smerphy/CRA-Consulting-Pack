The plan runs fourteen months from kick-off in October 2026. Phase 0, reporting readiness, is complete. Inventory and classification finish in December, and Wave 1 risk assessments by the end of February. Remediation is the longest phase, January to August 2027, followed by technical files. Conformity assessment runs from April to November and is the critical path: it depends on notified-body contracts by 31 March. Wave 1 carries the seven families that need a notified body plus the T200 pilot; Wave 2 reuses its templates for the default families; Wave 3 covers legacy lines. Three milestones are already at risk.
