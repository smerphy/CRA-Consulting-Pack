Illustrative · 13

Our response is an operating model, not thirteen separate projects. We build five shared capabilities once: the PSIRT and reporting process, the SBOM and supplier pipeline, hardened update infrastructure, a single system of record for evidence, and the policies and training that bind them. Each product family then runs the same six steps, from scope and class through risk assessment, remediation and the technical file to conformity assessment and the declaration. The T200 thermostat pilots the steps and produces the templates. Above it sit the CTO as accountable executive, a monthly programme board and named decision rights.
