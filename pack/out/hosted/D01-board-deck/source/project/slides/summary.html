We keep selling in the EU after 11 Dec 2027 if we act within two quarters

The case in four lines

Situation. The CRA makes cybersecurity a condition of CE marking. Reporting applies now; everything else from 11 Dec 2027.

Complication. Seven of 13 families, EUR 468m of EU revenue, need a notified body or a cited standard. Neither exists yet.

Question. What must we do, by when and at what cost, to keep selling in the EU after 11 Dec 2027?

Answer. Project Rampart: build shared capabilities once, apply them per product and book notified-body capacity by Q1 2027.

2

3

Compliance is an evidence system

Build once, prove it per product version for ten years; we start at 44% readiness.

If you read one slide, read this one. The situation: the CRA is in force and its reporting duty already covers every product we have sold. The complication: seven families carrying EUR 468m of EU revenue cannot be self-certified today, because no harmonised standard is cited and no notified body was designated at checks to 8 Sep 2026, and our readiness is 44%. Our answer is Project Rampart, a 14-month programme that builds shared capabilities once, applies them product by product and secures notified-body capacity by the end of Q1 2027. It costs EUR 9.3m to December 2027 and EUR 1.8m a year after that. We ask for five decisions today.
