The wider market is behind. Only 29% of German companies say they know what the CRA means for them. More than half of CRA-aware respondents in the Linux Foundation survey do not know the penalties. Most industrial firms expect longer development, and SBOM completeness is the top challenge reported to ENISA. These surveys vary in quality, so each is labelled by type and base. The implication is urgency, not comfort: when the market moves in 2027, notified-body slots and experienced product-security staff will be scarce. Moving now lets us book capacity before demand peaks.
