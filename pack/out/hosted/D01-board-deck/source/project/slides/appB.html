Illustrative · 21

The model is built bottom-up from eleven cost lines. The largest driver is engineering remediation: 26.2 FTE-years at a loaded EUR 120,000, sized by class. Notified-body fees are vendor estimates and the least certain input. The low case assumes harmonised standards are cited by mid-2027 and less remediation; the high case assumes scarcity forces a second body at premium fees, 30% more remediation and an extra programme FTE. Contingency rises from 10% to 20% across the cases. All figures are real 2026 euros excluding VAT; the full model is in the business case and cost model workbook (D10).
