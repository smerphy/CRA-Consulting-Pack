Appendix · A3 · Glossary and method

Twelve terms explain the deck; facts are verified to 30 Oct 2026

CRA

Regulation (EU) 2024/2847: cybersecurity rules for products with digital elements

Product with digital elements

Hardware or software that connects, plus the backends it needs to work

Class I and Class II

Important product categories in Annex III that need stricter conformity routes

Harmonised standard

EU standard cited in the Official Journal; applying it presumes conformity

Notified body

Independent assessor designated by a Member State and listed in NANDO

Module A, B+C, H

Internal control; type examination plus conformity to type; full quality assurance

Technical file

Annex VII evidence of conformity, kept 10 years or the support period if longer

Declaration of conformity (DoC)

Signed statement that a product meets the CRA; needed before CE marking

Support period

Period of vulnerability handling: at least five years unless use is shorter

SBOM

Software bill of materials: machine-readable component list; not public by default

PSIRT

Product security incident response team: handles vulnerabilities in our products

Single Reporting Platform (SRP)

ENISA’s portal for Art 14 notifications, live since 11 Sep 2026

Method. Facts verified to 30 Oct 2026 against the engagement knowledge base and an independent fact-check. Guidance C(2026) 5252 is a non-binding approved draft. Client data are illustrative; survey, vendor and commentary figures are labelled by type.

Source: EU Cyber Resilience Act — Engineering Compliance Knowledge Base and fact-check, 30 Oct 2026; EUR-Lex; Commission; ENISA
