Project Rampart · Board pre-read · 19 Nov 2026

Illustrative · 16

The base case is EUR 9.25m to December 2027, including 15% contingency: EUR 1.4m this quarter and EUR 7.8m in 2027. Almost half, EUR 4.4m, is engineering remediation, testing and risk assessments, sized at 26.2 FTE-years of remediation. Shared platforms cost under EUR 1m. From 2028 running costs are EUR 1.8m a year, mainly testing, the programme core and the PSIRT. The range is EUR 7.9m if harmonised standards are cited by mid-2027, to EUR 11.4m if notified-body scarcity forces a second body and premium fees. The one-off is 0.77% of one year’s group revenue, against EUR 468m of annual EU revenue that depends on it.
