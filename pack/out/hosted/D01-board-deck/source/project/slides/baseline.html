Illustrative · 11

We scored every family against 40 questions in ten domains, from 0 to 4, with level 3 as the target. The portfolio index is 44%. Reporting is our strongest domain at 72%, because we invested there first. The domains that decide market access are the weakest: documentation and declarations at 24%, conformity and market access at 16%. The industrial families, built on an IEC 62443 process, lead at around 50%. The two Class I home products, the L2 lock at 35% and the V1 doorbell at 38%, are among the least ready and face the hardest route.
