Illustrative · 6

This is our exposure on one page: thirteen product families, 55 SKUs, three business units, all in scope. Six are default products that we may self-assess, although two of them, HomeHub H3 and the EG-700 gateway, are borderline Class I pending a legal opinion due on 27 November. Six are Class I and one, the SG-900 firewall, is Class II; those seven need third-party assessment. In-scope EU revenue is EUR 807m of our EUR 824m total; the rest is SaaS revenue, which is not itself a product. Seven legacy lines, including 1.1 million T100 thermostats, also remain reportable.
