Project Rampart · Board pre-read · 19 Nov 2026

Illustrative · 18

Ten risks are on the register; five are rated high. The top one, and the only one rising, is notified-body capacity, which we mitigate through early pre-contracting and Module H. Second is engineering capacity: remediation competes with the 2027 roadmap for the same engineers, so the CTO proposes to ring-fence 26 FTE-years and defer two features. Third, no harmonised standard may be cited in time for Class I, so we plan on third-party routes. Fourth, supplier and SBOM gaps in firmware. Fifth, legacy lines still on sale, which BD5 resolves. Each risk has a named owner.
