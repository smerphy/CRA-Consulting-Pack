Illustrative · 3

The CRA in sixty seconds. Scope is broad: any product with digital elements sold in the EU whose intended or foreseeable use includes a data connection, direct or indirect. That covers our devices and firmware, our companion apps, software products such as ConfigStudio, our SDK, and our own backends where a product needs them. Internal-only tools, websites and browser-only services are out. The class decides the route to market. Default products self-assess under Module A. Class I products, such as our routers, switches, smart lock and video doorbell, need a notified body unless a harmonised standard is cited and applied in full; none is cited as at 30 Oct 2026. Class II, our SG-900 firewall, always needs third-party assessment. As manufacturer we carry almost every obligation: engineering builds the Annex I properties, PSIRT runs vulnerability handling and reporting, product management sets support periods, and compliance owns the technical file.
