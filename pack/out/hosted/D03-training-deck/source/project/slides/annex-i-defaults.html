The next three slides turn the thirteen items into patterns: what to build, and the acceptance test that proves it. Each test produces evidence for the technical file. The four items here are treated as near-universal in the draft standards, so expect to apply them. Item (a): ship without known exploitable vulnerabilities; we cover the release check in detail shortly. Item (b): no shared or default credentials, optional services off, and a reset that truly wipes data, credentials and cloud bindings. Item (d): authenticate every interface, including device-to-cloud and management paths, and surface possible unauthorised access; that reporting half is often missed. Item (j): justify every exposed interface in every product state, and lock debug ports in production. On the T200 that means per-unit pairing codes, no default account, a fused UART and Bluetooth pairing only during onboarding.
