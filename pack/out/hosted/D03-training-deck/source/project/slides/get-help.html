Where to get help. For anything that might be a vulnerability or active exploitation, call PSIRT on-call or use the PSIRT mailbox, and never sit on it. The contact details are placeholders in this version; the PSIRT lead confirms them before rollout. For everything else, use the Project Rampart channel. The table names the owner role for each topic: the product security officer for risk assessments, the release gate and Annex I patterns; the head of platform engineering for the SBOM pipeline and signing; the compliance lead for classification, the technical file and declarations; the PSIRT lead for reporting and drills; product management for support periods and user information. The knowledge base and its application implementation guide hold the detail behind every slide. Product teams can assess themselves with the CRA Readiness Diagnostic.
