First design question: where does our product end? The Commission guidance gives a three-element test for remote data processing. One, the processing happens at a distance, and edge, on-premises and private cloud all count. Two, without it the product cannot perform one of its functions; functions include sending commands, sync, onboarding, configuration, identity and update distribution. Three, we designed and developed it, or had it built solely to our specification. Elements two and three are decisive and cumulative; who operates it does not matter. Apply it to the T200. Our remote-control and update services are part of the product even though they run on third-party IaaS. The licensed weather API fails element three, so it is a component that needs due diligence instead. Analytics telemetry is not remote data processing, but data minimisation still applies. Record the result as a remote-function map.
