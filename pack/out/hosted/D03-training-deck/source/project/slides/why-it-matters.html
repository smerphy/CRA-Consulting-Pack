Illustrative · 2

Start with why. The Cyber Resilience Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, has been in force since 10 Dec 2024. One part is already live: since 11 Sep 2026, Article 14 requires us to report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents, starting with an early warning within 24 hours of becoming aware. It covers every in-scope product, even those sold years ago. Everything else applies from 11 Dec 2027, 406 days from 31 Oct 2026. From then, a product can only be placed on the EU market with a declaration of conformity and CE marking showing that the essential requirements are met. In the illustrative figures, EU revenue from our 13 in-scope families is EUR 807m of EUR 824m. The point for this room: proof comes from your daily work. Risk assessments, gate records, SBOMs and test reports become the technical file. Maximum fines reach EUR 15m or 2.5% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.
