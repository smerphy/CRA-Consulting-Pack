Illustrative · 17

Act on this slide. If you see signs that someone is exploiting one of our products, such as odd telemetry, a customer report or a researcher’s report, call PSIRT on-call within the hour. Do not wait for certainty: the incident commander decides when we are aware, after an immediate assessment. Preserve evidence: logs, dumps, samples and affected units. Note what you saw, in UTC. Do not disclose: no public tickets, posts or customer emails. Once we have a reasonable degree of certainty of exploitation, the clocks run: an early warning within 24 hours and a notification within 72 hours, then a final report 14 days after a fix is available, or one month after the notification for a severe incident. Remediation never pauses the clock. Researcher findings without exploitation evidence are not reportable on their own, but still go to PSIRT. Our first drill filed in 9h 40m.
