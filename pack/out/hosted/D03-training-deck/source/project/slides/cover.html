Welcome. This is the 45-minute core module of the half-day engineering training in Project Rampart, Example Group’s programme to be ready for the EU Cyber Resilience Act by 11 Dec 2027. It is written for engineers, tech leads, product managers, release managers and PSIRT staff, and it is practical: what changes in how we design, build, ship and support our products. We follow the lifecycle — design, build and release, ship and support, evidence — and use the T200 Smart Thermostat as the running example. A five-question knowledge check comes near the end, so keep notes. Facts are as at 30 Oct 2026; where guidance or standards are still drafts, the slide says so, and all Example Group figures are illustrative. Hold detailed questions for the end or post them in the programme channel. Our target is 60% of our 920 engineers trained by Mar 2027.
