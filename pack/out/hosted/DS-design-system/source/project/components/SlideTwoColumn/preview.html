01 · Reporting is already law

Reporting applies now; every other obligation lands on 11 Dec 2027

Already law · since 11 Sep 2026

Article 14 reporting, for every in-scope product

  • Early warning within 24 hours of awareness
  • Notification within 72 hours
  • Final report 14 days after a fix, or 1 month after notification for an incident
  • Covers products already on the market

From 11 Dec 2027

Everything else, per product and per version

  • Annex I essential requirements met and evidenced
  • Class I needs a cited standard or a notified body; Class II always third-party
  • Technical file and declaration kept for 10 years
  • Security updates for the support period, normally at least five years

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Arts 13, 14, 32 and 71; knowledge base, facts as at 30 Oct 2026
