The maximum fine for an Annex I breach at Example Group’s size is EUR 30m1, and the Commission’s guidance remains formally pending adoption2.

  1. Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Art 64(2): up to EUR 15m or 2.5% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher; 2.5% of EUR 1.2bn (illustrative revenue) is EUR 30m.
  2. Commission guidance C(2026) 5252, content approved 27 Jul 2026; non-binding and pending adoption in all languages, as at 30 Oct 2026.
