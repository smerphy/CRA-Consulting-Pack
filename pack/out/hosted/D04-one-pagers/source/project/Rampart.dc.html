03 · Governance

Three decisions cannot wait for a committee

Report

PSIRT lead, 24/7

Files the 24-hour early warning without prior approval; legal reviews the 72-hour report.

Accept residual risk

Product security officer

Cost, risk appetite and commercial strategy are not grounds; appeals go to the sponsor (BD4).

Sign the declaration

CEO or written delegate

Signs on a checklist the compliance lead counter-signs; the CTO is accountable executive (BD2).
