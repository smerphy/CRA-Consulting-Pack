Make it yours

Swap the illustrative client for a real one in five steps.

  1. Replace the client data first.Portfolio, revenue, classes and routes drive every number. Update the cost model inputs and the obligations register before touching the decks.
  2. Run the diagnostic with real product teams.Switch off the illustrative sample in the diagnostic; the portfolio heatmap and readiness index then come from the client's own answers.
  3. Re-cut the board and SteerCo decks.Keep the storyline and action titles; replace exhibits from the model, the diagnostic and the dashboard.
  4. Localise communications.Fill the bracketed placeholders, set approvers and dates, and check messages against the “say / don’t say” table.
  5. Re-date the fact base.Regulatory facts are verified to 30 Oct 2026. Re-check the watchlist before every client issue: standards, notified bodies, guidance and national laws move monthly.
