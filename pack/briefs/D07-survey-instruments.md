# Brief D07 — Survey and Interview Instruments (Claude Doc)

**Title:** "Project Rampart — Survey and Interview Instruments"
**Create from:** Docs type (`type_url: https://claude.ai/artifact/Rp9naXUCj2xozpUkyQy19W`), then write via the Claude Docs connector (`mcp__Claude_Docs__*`; read guide topics index, editing, tabs first). One tab per instrument.

**Purpose:** the research instruments a consultancy fields in the diagnostic phase of a CRA engagement, ready to copy into any survey tool (Microsoft Forms, Qualtrics, Google Forms) or run as interviews. Each instrument: purpose, audience, sampling, timing, length, data-protection note, the questions (numbered, with answer type and options), scoring/analysis method, and how results feed the programme.

**Inputs:** BRIEF.md; DATA_SUMMARY.md and `pack_data.json` → `diagnostic` (10 domains, 40 questions with basis, evidence and action — use them verbatim for instrument 2); corporate guide 06 (supplier clauses and questionnaire outline), 09 (training roles); knowledge base sections 03-06, 08-10, 12; research datapoints RD01-RD07 (for benchmark comparisons).

**Tabs:**
1. **Research design** — objectives (baseline readiness, awareness, supplier exposure, executive priorities, customer expectations); instrument map (who, how many, when, owner); fieldwork calendar (Nov-Dec 2026, aligned to Phase 1); data handling (GDPR: purpose limitation, pseudonymised staff responses, retention); analysis plan (readiness index definition; heatmaps; cut by BU/class/role; benchmark against published surveys); reporting outputs. Link to the live Readiness Diagnostic (D08) by name.
2. **Product readiness diagnostic (offline version)** — the 40 questions exactly as in `pack_data.json` (grouped by the 10 domains), the 0-4 scale with level descriptors, "evidence to attach" per question, scoring rules (readiness index = mean of min(score,3)/3), moderation guidance (how the programme challenges self-scores; evidence required for level 3+), and the target (level 3 by 11 Dec 2027; Art 14 domain now).
3. **Staff awareness pulse survey** — 15 questions, ~6 minutes: role and BU; knowledge checks with correct answers marked (e.g. when Art 14 reporting started; what an actively exploited vulnerability is; who can declare awareness; where to report a vulnerability; whether the SBOM must be public; what the support period minimum is); confidence and attitude Likert items; open text. Scoring: knowledge index; benchmark framing vs Bitkom's 29% "know what it means for them" (RD01) and LF's 56% unaware of penalties (RD02). Targets by role.
4. **Supplier CRA readiness questionnaire** — ~30 questions in the eight sections of corporate guide 06 (identity and role, conformity, vulnerability handling, SBOM, support, secure development, incident and reporting posture, evidence on request) with answer types; risk-based scoring rubric (critical/high/medium/low component) and thresholds that route a supplier to "approved / conditions / exit plan"; cover note referencing the supplier letter in the Communication Pack (D05).
5. **Executive and stakeholder interview guide** — 45-minute semi-structured guide: opening script, 12 core questions (exposure, risk appetite vs the product-risk rule, roadmap trade-offs, notified-body strategy, legacy lines, reporting authority, budget), role-specific probes (CTO, General Counsel, CFO, BU presidents, CISO, Head of quality & regulatory, Head of procurement), closing; plus an interview synthesis template (themes, quotes field, decisions implied, risks raised) and a consent line.
6. **Customer and channel survey** — 12 questions for B2B customers, integrators and distributors: what CRA evidence they will ask for (DoC, SBOM access, support period, CVD contact), timing, procurement clauses they plan, willingness to pay for extended support. Purpose: commercial planning and FAQ content.
7. **Engineering practice survey** — 15 questions for engineering leads on tooling and practice (SBOM generation method, binary SCA, signing and HSM use, update mechanism, fuzzing, release gate, CVD intake), used to size the shared-platform workstream.
8. **Analysis and reporting templates** — how to aggregate each instrument; example output tables (filled with the illustrative baseline from the data: portfolio index 44%, domain readiness indices, weakest questions); thresholds for red/amber/green; how results update the KPI pack.

**Quality:** professional survey design (neutral wording, one concept per question, balanced scales, "don't know" options where appropriate, mandatory vs optional flagged). Length roughly 7,000-10,000 words. Read back each tab before reporting.
